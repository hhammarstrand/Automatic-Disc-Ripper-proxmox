Storage

Where your finished files actually land — and how to send them to a NAS.

How the folders fit together 
/opt/adr/            the application — code, database, scratch space
  ├─ raw/            raw MKVs off the disc   (local, deleted after encoding)
  ├─ staging/        HandBrake writes here   (local, always)
  └─ completed/      finished films, if you have no NAS
/mnt/media/          finished films, if you do
Nothing is ever mounted inside /opt/adr — that directory belongs to the app. Your library is a separate filesystem mounted beside it, and Completed MP4 folder under Settings points at whichever of the two you are using.
