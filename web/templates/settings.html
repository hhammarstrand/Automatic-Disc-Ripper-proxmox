Duplicates

Before ripping, the disc is checked against three things: whether the film's folder already holds video at the destination (the only check that survives a cleared history), whether an earlier completed job has the same TMDb id (a different pressing of the same film), and finally the disc label. A duplicate is always logged and can be notified about.

Off by default. Re-ripping is legitimate when the first attempt came from a scratched disc or a worse preset, and a false positive that silently cancels a disc is harder to notice than one that only warns. Turn it on when working through a large shelf.
Television

A box-set disc has no "main feature" — several episodes of similar length instead. These decide what that looks like. They are a judgement, not a fact: anime runs to 24 minutes, a documentary series to 55, and some box set will sit outside any default. Detection only ever suggests; you confirm on the dashboard before encoding.

MakeMKV key

MakeMKV needs a registration key. While MakeMKV is in beta a free key is published on the forum and rotates roughly monthly. Click refresh to fetch the current beta key automatically, or paste your own purchased key.

Plex
Path to the Plex Movies folder, e.g. /mnt/plex/Movies. Leave empty to disable Plex move.
Plex keeps films and shows in separate libraries with different naming rules, so a season folder cannot go in the movie folder. Leave empty and series land in the completed folder instead.
Matched movies are automatically moved to the Plex folder when encoding is complete.
Without this the film is on disk but invisible in Plex until the next scheduled scan — which usually reads as the ripper having failed.
In Plex: open any item → Get Info → View XML, and copy X-Plex-Token out of the URL.
