    Optical drives
    
  

  

    

      The dashboard tells you whether a disc is loaded. This tells you whether the
      drive works — it opens the device, asks the drive for its tray status,
      checks the SCSI interface MakeMKV actually rips through, and reads a sector off
      the disc. Each step is reported on its own, because a drive that opens but
      refuses SG_IO looks perfectly healthy right up until a rip fails.

Checks that need the Proxmox host

  

    

      The device cgroup, the passthrough entries and guest-autostart ordering live in
      /etc/pve/lxc/{{ ctid or '<CTID>' }}.conf on the host. This container
      cannot read or change them — that is what container isolation is for — so it hands
      you the command instead of guessing. adr-doctor reports by default and
      only changes anything with --fix.
    

    

      adr-doctor --fix {{ ctid or '<CTID>' }}
